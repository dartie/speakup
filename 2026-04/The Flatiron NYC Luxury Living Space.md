Most of the world’s buildings are square or rectangular. So, naturally, when someone builds a triangular building, it gets a lot of attention, particularly if it is prominently positioned in one of the most famous cities in the world. [For over](## "per oltre") a century, the Flatiron Building in New York has been a symbol of the city. Now, for the first time, people will live inside the iconic triangular structure, which [is to open](## "è previsto che venga inaugurato") as a luxury apartment complex this year.

## AN ICONIC STRUCTURE
Designed by architect Daniel Burnham, the Flatiron Building first opened in 1902 as the [headquarters](## "sede") of the construction company Fuller Company. It was named the Fuller Building at the time, but became known as the Flatiron Building because of its triangular [shape](## "forma"). The building was reminiscent of the [flatiron](## "ferro da stiro"), an old, non-electrical version of a [clothes iron](## "ferro da stiro").
Situated on the intersection of Broadway and Fifth Avenue in Manhattan, the [twenty-two-storey building](## "edificio di ventidue piani") soon became famous worldwide, and the area around it became known as the Flatiron District. It was designated a New York City [landmark](## "luogo di interesse") in 1966, [added](## "aggiungere, incorporare") to the National Register of Historic Places in 1979, and designated a National Historic Landmark in 1989. Today, it is considered one of the most iconic buildings in the world.

## TRIANGULAR LIVING SPACES
The Flatiron Building was bought and sold several times over the years, and was used as the [headquarters](## "sede") of several different companies. However, in 2019, its final [corporate tenant](## "azienda locataria"), Macmillan Publishers, left, in part because the building was in desperate need of renovation. The iconic structure remained [vacant](## "vuota, non occupata"), its future uncertain, until a group led by New York [property developer](## "promotore immobiliare") Jeffrey Gural bought it in 2023.

## LUXURY
The group then began a massive project to convert the building from a century-old office space into a modern luxury apartment complex, at an estimated cost of [up to](## "fino a") $100 million. As part of the renovation, the building has been repaired, [restored](## "ristrutturare"), and modernised. Its old offices have been [replaced](## "sostituire") with around forty luxury apartments, while its [ground floor](## "piano terra") remains a commercial space, with new units and facades. Each apartment has distinctive triangular living spaces and a mix of historic and contemporary elements. They are expected to sell for between $11 and $50 million.
