Sam: The [CEO](## "amministratore delegato (Chief Executive Officer)") has asked our team to see if artificial intelligence can help us [increase](## "aumentare") productivity. [What are your thoughts?](## "cosa ne pensi?")
Janet: I think it could be really useful. AI can help organise our workload.
Sam: Organise it how?
Janet: Well, it could prioritise tasks and remind us about deadlines.
Sam: That would save a lot of time. Can it help with meetings too?
Janet: Yes, it can summarise meetings with [accurate](## "accurati") minutes.
Sam: That sounds almost too good to be true.
Janet: Actually, many companies already use [tools](## "strumenti") like that.
Sam: So, will AI [replace](## "sostituire") some of our staff?
Janet: [Not at all](## "per niente"). We’d just use it to take care of repetitive tasks.
Sam:So we can focus on more important work. And we would be more productive!
Janet: Exactly! AI should be used to support workers – not[replace](## "sostituire") them.

## NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!
Useful means helpful or practical.
Workload is the [amount](## "quantità") of work to be done.
A deadline is the time by which something must be finished.
To save time means to reduce the time needed to do something.
To summarise means to give the [main](## "principali") ideas in a short form.
The minutes are the official [records](## "verbali") of a meeting.
Too good to be true is an expression used when something seems unbelievable.
Actually is used to emphasise or correct information.
To [replace](## "sostituire") means to take the place of someone or something else.
To focus on means to give special attention to something.

# Glossary
* **CEO** = amministratore delegato (Chief Executive Officer)
* **increase** = aumentare
* **What are your thoughts?** = cosa ne pensi?
* **accurate** = accurati
* **tools** = strumenti
* **Not at all** = per niente
* **replace** = sostituire
* **amount** = quantità
* **main** = principali
* **records** = verbali
