Everyday Dialogues: AI For Workplace Productivity [B1]

Non guardiamo all’intelligenza artificiale come a un avversario pronto a sostituirci, ma come a un prezioso alleato che può aiutarci a migliorare la nostra produttività.

Sam: The CEO has asked our team to see if artificial intelligence can help us increase productivity. What are your thoughts? Janet: I think it could be really useful. AI can help organise our workload. Sam: Organise it how? Janet: Well, it could prioritise tasks and remind us about deadlines. Sam: That would save a lot of time. Can it help with meetings too? Janet: Yes, it can summarise meetings with accurate minutes. Sam: That sounds almost too good to be true. Janet: Actually, many companies already use tools like that. Sam: So, will AI replace some of our staff? Janet: Not at all. We’d just use it to take care of repetitive tasks. Sam:So we can focus on more important work. And we would be more productive! Janet: Exactly! AI should be used to support workers – notreplace them. Language

NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!

Useful means helpful or practical. Workload is the amount of work to be done. A deadline is the time by which something must be finished. To save time means to reduce the time needed to do something. To summarise means to give the main ideas in a short form. The minutes are the official records of a meeting. Too good to be true is an expression used when something seems unbelievable. Actually is used to emphasise or correct information. To replace means to take the place of someone or something else. To focus on means to give special attention to something.
