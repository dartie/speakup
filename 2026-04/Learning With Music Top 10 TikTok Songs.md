The world’s most downloaded app in the last five years, TikTok was launched less than a decade ago in China and rapidly changed how young people discover new music. Short [quirky](## "stravaganti") videos of dance moves or challenges are accompanied by [snippets](## "frammenti") of songs, creating a trend where users upload their own versions. As the videos become viral, so do the songs.
Many [savvy](## "astuti") artists have realised the influence of the app, most famously rapper Lil Nas X, whose Old Town Road spent a record nineteen weeks at Number One in the US in 2019 after he launched an online challenge. Last year the platform [claimed](## "affermare") that “84 per cent of songs that entered the Billboard Global 200 in 2024 went viral on TikTok first.” It has, more notably, revived the fortunes of older songs, both famous and obscure — its most streamed song of 2025 was Connie Francis’ Pretty Little Baby, an almost unknown album track recorded over six decades earlier.

## 1. PRETTY LITTLE BABY, CONNIE FRANCIS (1962)
American singer Connie Francis was hugely successful in the early days of pop music but Pretty Little Baby was an [overlooked](## "trascurare, ignorare") album track. This changed last year when users and influencers such as Kim Kardashian and Kylie Jenner began to upload videos [lipsynching](## "cantare in playback, sincronizzare le labbra") to [cute](## "adorabili") babies. The lyrics, almost all in the present simple and continuous, are a simple expression of young love. The singer, who passed away last July, was [delighted](## "entusiasta") with the resurgence but admitted that not only had she never heard of TikTok, she had forgotten the song existed!
You can ask the flowers, I sit for hours
Tellin’ all the [bluebirds](## "uccellini"),
the [bill and coo birds](## "uccelli che strofinano il becco e tubano")
Pretty little baby
I’m so in love with you

## 2. TURN THE LIGHTS OFF, KATO (2010)
A TikTok meme of American actor Jon Hamm dancing to [Danish](## "danese") disc jockey Kato’s Turn The Lights Off in the Apple TV series Your Friends & Neighbors [spawned](## "dare origine a") a [slew](## "gran quantità") of [copycat](## "copie, imitatori") videos in 2025. It subsequently became a club favourite and climbed the international charts. The lyrics open with two imperatives and an often-used abbreviation of ‘because’.
I said ooh-ooh
Come on, baby, turn the lights off
Ooh-ooh
‘Cause it’s getting late

## 3. HARNESS YOUR HOPES, PAVEMENT (1999)
An unlikely viral hit, this was a B-side for American indie band Pavement, who [disbanded](## "sciogliersi") the same year. Singer Stephen Malkmus discovered the song’s [belated](## "tardivo") 2020 success when he heard it playing in a local [bakery](## "panetteria") and was told TikTok users were dancing to it. This led to a successful reunion tour for the band. The song had a second resurgence in 2024 when it accompanied users showing off their [outfits](## "abbinamenti"). The lyrics have examples of positive and negative imperatives. 
[Harness](## "incanalare") your hopes
On just one person
Because you know a [harness](## "imbracatura, cintura")
Was only made for one
Don’t telegraph your passes
You’ll end up with [molasses](## "melassa")

## 4. BLOODY MARY, LADY GAGA (2010)
Although not featured in the 2022 Netflix comedy horror series Wednesday, fans began to recreate the dance scene using a [sped-up](## "accelerata") version of this Lady Gaga album track. Its popularity [prompted](## "provocare") its [release](## "uscita") as a single twelve years after its recording. The lyrics contrast the initial spontaneity of the future ‘will’ with the ‘going to’ for intentions, as well as the informal use of “like someone said” instead of “as someone said.”
I’ll dance, dance, dance
With my hands, hands, hands
Above my head, head, head
Like Jesus said
I’m gonna dance, dance, dance
With my hands, hands, hands
above my head

## 5. FOREVER YOUNG, ALPHAVILLE (1984)
The third single by German band Alphaville has soundtracked various trending videos, from slow-motion footage of couples [swinging](## "oscillare") a water bottle to montages of users getting older. The band’s singer posted a video of scenes of his daughter growing up, saying he “[hopped on the beam](## "unirsi al trend")” (meaning he joined the trend.) The song’s chorus features an example of a yes/no question with auxiliary.
So many dreams [swinging](## "oscillare") [out of the blue](## "all’improvviso, dal nulla")We let them come true
Forever young, I want to be forever young
Do you really want to live forever, forever and ever?

## 6. YOUNGEST DAUGHTER, SUPERHEAVEN (2013)
This album track revived the fortunes of semi-retired American alternative rock band Superheaven. Its emotional lyrics, almost entirely in the present simple, [resonated with](## "entrare in sintonia con") youngest daughters when it was featured in several videos a decade after its [release](## "uscita"). It became the band’s most-streamed song and [prompted](## "provocare") them to [resume](## "riprendere") touring.
It’s useless, I tried, but [to no avail](## "inutilmente")To tell you how much I know,
how much I care

## 7. APPLE, CHARLI XCX (2024)
British singer Charli xcx’s last album “[Brat](## "monella, viziata, sfacciata")“ was a huge success, partly because of an aggressive marketing campaign but mainly thanks to TikTok. New York-based influencer Kelley Heyer felt that one track, Apple, was not getting the attention it [deserved](## "meritare") and created a set of dance moves. These soon became viral and the song [shot into](## "balzare, schizzare in") the charts. The lyrics are in the present simple with examples of informal English. 
I think the apple’s [rotten](## "marcia")[right to the core](## "fino al midollo")
From all the things passed down
From all the apples coming before
I [split](## "dividere") the apple down symmetrical lines
And what I find is kinda scary
Makes me just wanna drive

## 8. DREAMS, FLEETWOOD MAC (1977)
In 2020 a middle-aged American filmed himself skateboarding while drinking from a juice bottle, [mouthing](## "muovere le labbra, fare finta di cantare") this classic song. The twenty-two-second video immediately went viral and the song was streamed a record number of times, returning to the charts. Users [rushed](## "precipitarsi") to upload their own versions, including the band’s seventy-three-year-old drummer Mick Fleetwood! The [deceptively](## "apparentemente") simple lyrics feature useful expressions and idioms.
Now here you go again
You say you want your freedom
Well, who am I to [keep you down](## "reprimere, trattenere")It’s only right that you should
Play the way you feel it

## 9. KILLING ME SOFTLY, FUGEES (1996)
When American hip-hop group Fugees recorded this cover, already a huge hit for Roberta Flack in 1973, it became 1996’s [biggest-selling](## "più venduto") single worldwide. It has [swept through](## "diffondersi") TikTok several times — one remix accompanied a [shuffle dance](## "danza shuffle") trend and another was part of a walking challenge. The title features an adverb and the chorus is in the present continuous, giving immediacy and intimacy to the song.
[Strumming](## "strimpellare") my pain with his fingers
Singing my life with his words 
Killing me softly with his song
Killing me softly with his song
Telling my whole life with his words
Killing me softly with his song

## 10. RUNNING UP THAT HILL, KATE BUSH (1985)
Now forever associated with Netflix series Stranger Things, this 80s single went viral in 2022 and gave Kate Bush her second UK number one a record forty-four years after her first (with Wuthering Heights.) Highlighting the platform’s musical importance, one report [claimed](## "affermare") that 39 per cent of Generation Z only recognise the song from TikTok. The chorus features an example of the second conditional. 
And if I only could
I’d [make a deal](## "fare un patto") with God
And I’d get Him to [swap](## "scambiare") our places
I’d be runnin’ up that road
Be runnin’ up that hill
With no problems
