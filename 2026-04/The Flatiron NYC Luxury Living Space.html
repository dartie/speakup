Most of the world’s buildings are square or rectangular. So, naturally, when someone builds a triangular building, it gets a lot of attention, particularly if it is prominently positioned in one of the most famous cities in the world. For over a century, the Flatiron Building in New York has been a symbol of the city. Now, for the first time, people will live inside the iconic triangular structure, which is to open as a luxury apartment complex this year.

AN ICONIC STRUCTURE

Designed by architect Daniel Burnham, the Flatiron Building first opened in 1902 as the headquarters of the construction company Fuller Company. It was named the Fuller Building at the time, but became known as the Flatiron Building because of its triangular shape. The building was reminiscent of the flatiron, an old, non-electrical version of a clothes iron. Situated on the intersection of Broadway and Fifth Avenue in Manhattan, the twenty-two-storey building soon became famous worldwide, and the area around it became known as the Flatiron District. It was designated a New York City landmark in 1966, added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1979, and designated a National Historic Landmark in 1989. Today, it is considered one of the most iconic buildings in the world. Luxury condo for sale in the Flatiron Building, NYC.

TRIANGULAR LIVING SPACES

The Flatiron Building was bought and sold several times over the years, and was used as the headquarters of several different companies. However, in 2019, its final corporate tenant, Macmillan Publishers, left, in part because the building was in desperate need of renovation. The iconic structure remained vacant, its future uncertain, until a group led by New York property developer Jeffrey Gural bought it in 2023.  Places

LUXURY

The group then began a massive project to convert the building from a century-old office space into a modern luxury apartment complex, at an estimated cost of up to \(100 million. As part of the renovation, the building has been repaired, [restored](## "ristrutturare"), and modernised. Its old offices have been [replaced](## "sostituire") with around forty luxury apartments, while its [ground floor](## "piano terra") remains a commercial space, with new units and facades. Each apartment has distinctive triangular living spaces and a mix of historic and contemporary elements. They are expected to sell for between \)11 and $50 million.
