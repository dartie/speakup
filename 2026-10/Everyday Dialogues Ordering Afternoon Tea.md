# Everyday Dialogues: Ordering Afternoon Tea   [B1]

Andare in una 'tea room' e godersi un 'afternoon tea' è una delle esperienze più autentiche che si possano fare nel Regno Unito. Ecco come chiederlo.

Waiter: Welcome to the Tea Room. Do you have a reservation with us today?
Alex: Yes, I reserved a table for two at 3pm under the name of Smith.
Waiter: Excellent. Right this way, please. Would you like to start with a glass of champagne?
Alex: No, we’ll stick to tea, thank you. Could you recommend a good black tea?
Waiter: We have a number of varieties and blends, but I personally love the Darjeeling or a classic Earl Grey.
Alex: Okay , I’ll have an Earl Grey and my friend would like a green tea.
Waiter: Wonderful. Will you both have the standard afternoon tea menu?
Alex: Can you tell us what’s included?
Waiter: Of course. It’s the traditional tiered stand of pastries, sandwiches and scones, with clotted cream and jam. There are also gluten-free and vegan options.
Alex: Amazing! Yes, we’ll have the [regular](## "normale, tradizionale") one, please.
Waiter: And do you have any dietary restrictions or food allergies?
Alex: No, we don’t.
Waiter: Perfect. I’ll bring your [pots](## "teiere") of tea out now and the food will follow shortly.

## NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!
When you make a booking or reservation, you do it "under the name of" the person concerned (usually yourself.)
"Right this way" is a polite English phrase used to guide someone in a specific direction.
The phrasal verb 'to stick' to means to do (or continue doing) something without changing to anything else.
"A blend of tea" is a mixture of two or more ingredients to create a beverage with unique flavours and health benefits.
Darjeeling is a premium black tea grown in the Himalayas of West Bengal, India, often called the 'Champagne of teas'.
Earl Grey is a popular black tea flavoured with bergamot oil.
A tiered stand is a structure with two or more plates connected to a central post. (A tier is a level or [layer](## "strato").)
Pastries are [baked goods](## "prodotti da forno") that are [savoury](## "salati") or sweet, such as croissants, profiteroles or éclairs.
A scone is a traditional British [quick bread](## "pane veloce (a lievitazione istantanea)") and a key part of afternoon tea. 
Clotted cream is a very [thick](## "spessa") cream made with full-fat [cow’s milk](## "latte vaccino"), traditionally from Devon and Cornwall. It is often [spread](## "spalmare") on scones, along with strawberry jam.
"Dietary restrictions" refer to the limitations on the foods a person can eat for health, ethical or religious reasons.
'Shortly' is a synonym for 'soon'.

# Glossary
* **spread** = spalmare
* **baked goods** = prodotti da forno
* **savoury** = salati
* **quick bread** = pane veloce (a lievitazione istantanea)
* **thick** = spessa
* **regular** = normale, tradizionale
* **pots** = teiere
* **layer** = strato
* **cow’s milk** = latte vaccino
