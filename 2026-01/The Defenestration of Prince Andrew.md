# The Defenestration of Prince Andrew   [C1]

A causa del suo coinvolgimento nello scandalo Epstein, il Principe Andrea, privato dei titoli, è stato costretto a lasciare la residenza di Windsor. D’ora in poi, il fratello del re si chiamerà Andrew Mountbatten Windsor.

Prince Andrew is to be [stripped](## "privare, togliere") of his royal titles and will [move out](## "andarsene, trasferirsi") of his home at the Royal [Lodge](## "residenza") in Windsor, Buckingham Palace has announced.
King Charles has initiated a “formal process to [remove](## "rimuovere, togliere") the [style](## "trattamento d’onore"), titles and honours of Prince Andrew,” who will now be known as Andrew Mountbatten Windsor, the Palace said.
It is understood the King had the support of the Prince of Wales in the decision and Andrew did not object to the process.
The decision follows anxiety within the royal [household](## "famiglia, nucleo domestico") about the reputational risk to the monarchy caused by continual headlines concerning Andrew’s friendship with the [late](## "defunto") child sex offender Jeffrey Epstein and [allegations](## "accuse") of sexual assault against him by one of Epstein’s victims, Virginia Giuffre.
In October, Giuffre, who died by suicide in April of last year, aged forty-one, published her posthumous memoir, titled Nobody’s Girl: A Memoir of Surviving Abuse and Fighting for Justice. In the book she claimed the Prince “believed that having sex with me was his [birthright](## "diritto di nascita").”
Andrew has always denied [claims](## "affermazioni") he had sex with Giuffre when she was seventeen, and [settled a civil case](## "chiudere, risolvere una causa civile") with her for a reported £12m with no admission of [liability](## "responsabilità").
Giuffre’s family said on Thursday that “today, she declares a victory” and that she had “brought down a British prince with her truth and extraordinary courage.”
Buckingham Palace said in a statement: “Prince Andrew will now be known as Andrew Mountbatten Windsor.
His [lease](## "locazione") on Royal [Lodge](## "residenza") has, to date, provided him with legal protection to continue in residence. Formal notice has now been served to [surrender](## "cedere, consegnare") the [lease](## "locazione") and he will move to alternative private [accommodation](## "alloggio, sistemazione"). These [censures](## "misure") are [deemed](## "ritenere") necessary, [notwithstanding](## "nonostante") the fact that he continues to deny the [allegations](## "accuse") against him.
Their majesties wish to make clear that their thoughts and [utmost](## "massime, tutte") sympathies have been, and will remain with, the victims and survivors of any and all forms of abuse.”
It is understood that Andrew will move to a property on the private Sandringham estate in Norfolk, to be privately funded by the King.
His ex-wife, Sarah Ferguson, will also [move out](## "andarsene, trasferirsi") of Royal [Lodge](## "residenza") and will [sort out](## "risolvere, sistemare") her own [living arrangements](## "sistemazioni abitative").
Formal notice was given to [surrender](## "cedere, consegnare") the [lease](## "locazione") at the Royal [Lodge](## "residenza") on Thursday and it is understood that Andrew’s move to Sandringham will take place “as soon as practicable.” He will receive a private [provision](## "provvigione") from the King, with any other [sources of income](## "fonti di reddito") to be a matter for the [former duke](## "ex duca").
The removal process applies to the titles of Prince, Duke of York, [Earl](## "conte") of Inverness, Baron Killyleagh, and the [style](## "trattamento d’onore") His Royal [Highness](## "altezza"). The honours affected are Andrew’s Order of the [Garter](## "giarrettiera") and Knight Grand Cross of the Royal Victorian Order. He had ceased to use the [HRH](## "Sua Altezza Reale (His Royal [Highness](## "altezza"))") [style](## "trattamento d’onore") in 2022 but it had not been formally [remove](## "rimuovere, togliere")d.
As daughters of the son of a monarch, Princess Beatrice and Princess Eugenie retain their titles in line with King George V’s [letters patent](## "lettere patenti") of 1917.
The King is understood to have acted now because while Andrew continues to deny the accusations against him, it is felt that there have been serious [lapses of judgment](## "errori, mancanze di giudizio").
The royal family had announced on 17 October that Andrew would voluntarily stop using the title Duke of York and give up his honours as a Knight Grand Cross of the Royal Victorian Order and Royal Knight Companion of the Most Noble Order of the [Garter](## "giarrettiera").
The [dukedom](## "ducato, titolo di duca") of York is a [peerage](## "titolo nobiliare"). The King sent [royal warrants](## "ordinanze reali") to the lord chancellor to secure the removal of the [dukedom](## "ducato, titolo di duca") from the [[peerage](## "titolo nobiliare") roll](## "lista, registro dei titoli nobiliari"), and the title of prince and [style](## "trattamento d’onore") of Royal [Highness](## "altezza"). The subsidiary titles of Inverness and Killyleagh are similarly affected.
The move is understood to have taken place in consultation with the relevant government authorities. The government supports the decision.
In a statement to the BBC, Giuffre’s family said: “Today, an ordinary American girl from an ordinary American family brought down a British prince with her truth and extraordinary courage.
Virginia Roberts Giuffre, our sister, a child when she was sexually assaulted by Andrew, never stopped fighting for [accountability](## "responsabilità") for what had happened to her and countless other survivors like her.
Today, she declares a victory. We, her family, along with her survivor sisters, continue Virginia’s battle and will not rest until the same [accountability](## "responsabilità") applies to all of her abusers and [abetters](## "complici"), connected to Jeffrey Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell.”
Sky Roberts, Giuffre’s brother, [commended](## "elogiare") the King for “setting a precedent” and thanked him for the mention of “victims and survivors of any and all forms of abuse” in the statement, but said Andrew should be put “behind bars.”
Giuffre [alleged](## "sostenere") she was forced to have sex with Andrew three times — once at the convicted sex trafficker Maxwell’s home in London, once at Epstein’s address in Manhattan, and once on the disgraced financier’s private island, Little St. James. Andrew has always denied the [allegations](## "accuse").
The Conservative leader, Kemi Badenoch, said it must have been “very difficult” for the King to take the steps against his [sibling](## "fratello"), but that it was right for the public not to tolerate sexual abuse [allegations](## "accuse").
The culture secretary, Lisa Nandy, told the BBC that removing Andrew’s titles was a “really brave, important and right step” by the King, which sends a “powerful message” to sexual abuse victims.
The Democratic congressman Suhas Subramanyam, who has previously [called for](## "richiedere, sollecitare") Andrew to testify before a US Congressional committee about his links to Epstein and Maxwell, [urged](## "esortare") Andrew to give evidence.
He said: “It’s clear that Prince Andrew has information about Epstein’s crimes and he must do more than just give up titles or hide from the public [spotlight](## "riflettore"). He [owes](## "dovere, essere in debito") it to the victims to share everything he knows about Epstein’s criminal operation and come before the [oversight committee](## "comitato di supervisione").
“[Regardless](## "a prescindere"), we will continue to [pursue](## "perseguire, portare avanti") the files and all the evidence, no matter how rich and powerful the perpetrators involved.”  
Published in The Guardian on 30 October 2025. Reprinted with permission.
