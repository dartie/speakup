Tom: Good morning! I’m here for my appraisal.
Jane: Hi, Tom. Please sit down while I review your self-assessment form. Hmmm. You’ve had a [busy](## "impegnativo") year!
Tom: Yes, I have. My line manager has told you all about it, right?
Jane: She has. She also said our [policy](## "politica") of continuous feedback has helped to keep your progress on track.
Tom: It’s been really useful with my personal development goals, especially [time management](## "gestione del tempo").
Jane: Ah, yes. She says you’ve really done well organising your [schedule](## "programma, agenda").
Tom: Thanks! I also did some training to boost my negotiation techniques.
Jane: And look what happened! You closed the biggest deal in our history.
Tom: Well, it was a [team effort](## "lavoro di squadra"). I’m really proud of how we worked together on that.
Jane: Teamwork is a critical soft skill, not only for you, but also for the business.
Tom: Absolutely! I’ve learned a lot from my manager about communication and active listening.
Jane: That’s good. Anything you want to work on going forward?
Tom: Of course! I want to expand my professional network and manage my own team.
Jane: You’re ambitious! Well, keep going as you are and you’ll do very well. Now let’s go through your self assessment and discuss your objectives.

## NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!
An appraisal is a formal assessment of the [performance](## "prestazione, rendimento") of an employee.
A self-assessment form is a document used by an employee to evaluate their own job [performance](## "prestazione, rendimento"), [achievements](## "risultati") and areas for [improvement](## "miglioramento").
A line manager is the person directly responsible for managing the work of an employee one level below them in the corporate hierarchy.
Continuous feedback is a process where an employee receives [ongoing](## "continuo, costante") feedback and is guided in a systematic manner.
On track is another way of saying ‘in the direction that was intended’.
Personal development goals are objectives for self-[improvement](## "miglioramento") in areas like [skills](## "competenze") and knowledge.
To do well means to be successful.
To boost means to improve.
You close a deal when you officially finalise a business agreement or negotiation, typically by signing a contract.
Soft [skills](## "competenze") are personal attributes that [enable](## "rendere possibile") someone to interact effectively and harmoniously with other people.
Active listening is a communication technique where the listener [engages](## "coinvolgere") with the speaker and [summarises](## "riassumere") what they say, to indicate understanding.
Going forward is another way of saying ‘from now on’ or ‘in the future’.
Keep going as you are is a phrase used to [encourage](## "incoraggiare") someone to continue doing something, because it is working well.
