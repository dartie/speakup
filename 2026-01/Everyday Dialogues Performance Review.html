Everyday Dialogues: Performance Review [B2]

In questo breve dialogo ripassiamo alcuni termini utili in ambito lavorativo, soprattutto per valorizzare i vostri risultati in vista del prossimo incontro con il team delle risorse umane.

Tom: Good morning! I’m here for my appraisal. Jane: Hi, Tom. Please sit down while I review your self-assessment form. Hmmm. You’ve had a busy year! Tom: Yes, I have. My line manager has told you all about it, right? Jane: She has. She also said our policy of continuous feedback has helped to keep your progress on track. Tom: It’s been really useful with my personal development goals, especially time management. Jane: Ah, yes. She says you’ve really done well organising your schedule. Tom: Thanks! I also did some training to boost my negotiation techniques. Jane: And look what happened! You closed the biggest deal in our history. Tom: Well, it was a team effort. I’m really proud of how we worked together on that. Jane: Teamwork is a critical soft skill, not only for you, but also for the business. Tom: Absolutely! I’ve learned a lot from my manager about communication and active listening. Jane: That’s good. Anything you want to work on going forward? Tom: Of course! I want to expand my professional network and manage my own team. Jane: You’re ambitious! Well, keep going as you are and you’ll do very well. Now let’s go through your self assessment and discuss your objectives.

NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!

An appraisal is a formal assessment of the performance of an employee. A self-assessment form is a document used by an employee to evaluate their own job performance, achievements and areas for improvement. A line manager is the person directly responsible for managing the work of an employee one level below them in the corporate hierarchy. Continuous feedback is a process where an employee receives ongoing feedback and is guided in a systematic manner. On track is another way of saying ‘in the direction that was intended’. Personal development goals are objectives for self-improvement in areas like skills and knowledge. To do well means to be successful. To boost means to improve. You close a deal when you officially finalise a business agreement or negotiation, typically by signing a contract. Soft skills are personal attributes that enable someone to interact effectively and harmoniously with other people. Active listening is a communication technique where the listener engages with the speaker and summarises what they say, to indicate understanding. Going forward is another way of saying ‘from now on’ or ‘in the future’. Keep going as you are is a phrase used to encourage someone to continue doing something, because it is working well.
