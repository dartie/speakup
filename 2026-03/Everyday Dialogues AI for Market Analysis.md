James: The boss wants us to use artificial intelligence for market analysis. Any ideas?
Anne: Well, we could use it to [collect](## "raccogliere") data like customer reviews.
James: How would that work?
Anne: We’d need an AI [tool](## "strumento") that can scan websites and social media.
James: Yes, I can imagine a [tool](## "strumento") like that could do that very quickly. What about analysing that data?
Anne: AI can do that, too. It’s great at finding [patterns](## "schemi, modelli"), like what customers like or dislike.
James: Wow, can it see the future, too? 
Anne: Actually, yes! AI can predict [trends](## "tendenze") by studying past and [current](## "attuali") data.
James: It sounds like human beings aren’t needed anymore.
Anne: Oh, but they are! AI helps a lot, but humans should always review the results.
James: Why is that?
Anne: Because AI is known to hallucinate!
James: You’re kidding!

## NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!
The boss is the person [in charge](## "responsabile, al comando"), like a [CEO](## "amministratore delegato (Chief Executive Officer)"), manager or supervisor. 
Any ideas? is a casual way of asking for suggestions.
An AI [tool](## "strumento") is a software application that uses algorithms like machine learning to perform tasks.
Like that means ‘of that kind’. You can also use ‘such’: ‘such a [tool](## "strumento")...’
To be good/great at indicates [proficiency](## "competenza"). It is always followed by a [noun](## "nome, sostantivo") or a gerund (in this case, ‘finding’).
Wow! is an informal exclamation of [astonishment](## "stupore") .
Actually is used to emphasise that something is true.
You’re kidding! is an informal exclamation expressing [disbelief](## "incredulità"), surprise or shock, similar to “No way!” ‘To kid’ means ‘to joke’.

# Glossary
* **collect** = raccogliere
* **patterns** = schemi, modelli
* **current** = attuali
* **in charge** = responsabile, al comando
* **CEO** = amministratore delegato (Chief Executive Officer)
* **tool** = strumento
* **noun** = nome, sostantivo
* **trends** = tendenze
* **proficiency** = competenza
* **astonishment** = stupore
* **disbelief** = incredulità
