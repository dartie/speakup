Everyday Dialogues: AI for Market Analysis [B1]

L’intelligenza artificiale è destinata a sostituire il lavoro degli umani? La risposta è no, ma può essere un valido aiuto.

James: The boss wants us to use artificial intelligence for market analysis. Any ideas? Anne: Well, we could use it to collect data like customer reviews. James: How would that work? Anne: We’d need an AI tool that can scan websites and social media. James: Yes, I can imagine a tool like that could do that very quickly. What about analysing that data? Anne: AI can do that, too. It’s great at finding patterns, like what customers like or dislike. James: Wow, can it see the future, too?  Anne: Actually, yes! AI can predict trends by studying past and current data. James: It sounds like human beings aren’t needed anymore. Anne: Oh, but they are! AI helps a lot, but humans should always review the results. James: Why is that? Anne: Because AI is known to hallucinate! James: You’re kidding! Language

NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!

The boss is the person in charge, like a CEO, manager or supervisor.  Any ideas? is a casual way of asking for suggestions. An AI tool is a software application that uses algorithms like machine learning to perform tasks. Like that means ‘of that kind’. You can also use ‘such’: ‘such a tool…’ To be good/great at indicates proficiency. It is always followed by a noun or a gerund (in this case, ‘finding’). Wow! is an informal exclamation of astonishment . Actually is used to emphasise that something is true. You’re kidding! is an informal exclamation expressing disbelief, surprise or shock, similar to “No way!” ‘To kid’ means ‘to joke’.

Glossary

  • patterns = schemi, modelli
  • trends = tendenze
  • CEO = amministratore delegato (Chief Executive Officer)
  • tool = strumento
  • astonishment = stupore
  • disbelief = incredulità
  • collect = raccogliere
  • current = attuali
  • in charge = responsabile, al comando
  • proficiency = competenza
  • noun = nome, sostantivo
