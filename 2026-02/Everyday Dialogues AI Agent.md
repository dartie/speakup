John: So, artificial intelligence has taken the burden off the customer service department.
Sarah: Yes, the AI agent has reduced the workload a lot.
John: I understand we had some initial hiccups?
Sarah: Yes. It can handle standard [enquiries](## "richieste"), but anything complex or out of the ordinary can cause problems.
John: What happens if it can’t resolve an issue?
Sarah: Then it [escalates](## "inoltrare") the enquiry to a human agent.
John: But it’s getting better, right?
Sarah: Yes. Machine learning means it can deal with most calls now. The answers are also getting more detailed and [accurate](## "precise").
John: What’s the resolution rate at the moment?
Sarah: It’s 65 per cent, which is very good.
John: Do we have any feedback from our customers?
Sarah: Yes, they really like the live chat function. Average ratings are [four out of five](## "quattro su cinque").
John: Great. Given it’s success, we might roll it out to other areas of the business.

## NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!
When you free someone or something from a lot of work, you take the burden off them.
Workload is the total [amount](## "quantità") of work to be done.
The word hiccups can be used to refer to small, temporary problems that are easily [fixed](## "risolvere").
To handle is a synonym for ‘to manage’.
Out of the ordinary means unusual.
An issue is an important [topic](## "questione, argomento"). It is often used as a synonym for ‘problem’.
Machine learning refers to the use of algorithms and statistical models that allow computer systems to learn and adapt without explicit instructions.
To deal with something, often a problem, means to take action to resolve it.
Resolution rate is the percentage of customer [support tickets](## "ticket, richieste di assistenza") or service requests that are fully resolved within a specific time period.
Feedback is information about the reactions to a product or service, used as the basis for improvement.
Live chat is real-time, text-based online communication between people or, in this case, between a customer and the AI agent.
Ratings are a measurement of how good or popular something is.
Used as a preposition, given means ‘[taking into account](## "prendere in considerazione")’ or ‘considering’.
To roll out is the same as ‘to introduce’ or ‘to launch’.

# Glossary
* **enquiries** = richieste
* **four out of five** = quattro su cinque
* **amount** = quantità
* **support tickets** = ticket, richieste di assistenza
* **escalates** = inoltrare
* **accurate** = precise
* **fixed** = risolvere
* **topic** = questione, argomento
* **taking into account** = prendere in considerazione
