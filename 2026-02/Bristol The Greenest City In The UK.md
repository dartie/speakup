Back in 2015, when the UK was part of the EU, the city of Bristol in the south-west of England was [awarded](## "premiare") the title of European Green Capital. Ten years on, the city has been included in a list of the twenty most eco-friendly travel destinations, compiled by global travel site Big 7 Travel. Bristol is also the UK’s first Cycling City, the UK’s second Gold Sustainable Food City and a [fair-trade](## "commercio equo e solidale") city. With all these green [accolades](## "riconoscimenti"), [it is no wonder](## "non c’è da meravigliarsi") that the city is home to a number of organisations with an environmental focus. Sustrans, a sustainable transport [NGO](## "ONG (Non-Governmental Organization)"), and the [Soil](## "suolo") Association, a charity working to revolutionise farming, eating practices, and the use of natural resources, are just two examples.

## GREEN SPACES AND PATHS
So what is it that makes Bristol a great place to visit for the environmentally-conscious? Aside from green spaces in the form of over four hundred parks and gardens, Bristol also [boasts](## "vantare") city farms and thirty nature reserves in the city and surrounding area, including [salt marshes](## "paludi salmastre") and wildflower [grasslands](## "praterie"). Being a city that prioritises sustainable travel, Bristol is easily accessible to those on foot or travelling by bike or [e-scooter](## "monopattino elettrico"). Many visitors and locals enjoy the Bristol to Bath Railway [Path](## "percorso"), a 13-mile (20.9-kilometre) route that is available to cyclists, walkers and disabled users alike.

## PLANT-BASED
Bristol is also a [haven](## "rifugio, oasi") for those looking for sustainable food, drink and shopping options. Named the world’s vegan capital by food lovers’ website Chef’s Pencil, Bristol is a city with innumerable plant-based dining options. Restaurants such as The Canteen, a purely vegan and vegetarian restaurant, offer guests seasonal options and plant-based versions of classic British dishes, such as Vegan Wellington (in place of the traditional Beef Wellington.) And [savvy](## "esperti") shoppers will enjoy [browsing](## "curiosare") second-hand clothing and other items in the city — there are around 145 charity shops and sixteen [car boot sales](## "mercatini dell’usato") to enjoy.

## EDUCATION
For those looking to educate themselves on environmental issues while in the city, it is well worth visiting the Create Centre and Ecohome, where a variety of events and exhibitions with a focus on sustainability are offered all year round. The Ecohome displays how houses can be built with nature in mind, integrating environmentally-friendly construction methods. We The Curious, a Bristol-based science centre, is also a must-see attraction. In 2019, this interactive museum declared a climate emergency and promised to become [carbon net zero](## "senza emissioni nette di carbonio") by 2030. So, in short, if you’re looking to visit a green city in the UK, Bristol should be at the top of your list!
