Bristol: The Greenest City In The UK [B1]

Da ormai parecchi anni, la città è famosa per il suo occhio di riguardo nei confronti dell’ambiente. Dai ristoranti vegani e vegetariani allo shopping, passando per il trasporto pubblico: la parola d’ordine è sostenibilità.

Back in 2015, when the UK was part of the EU, the city of Bristol in the south-west of England was awarded the title of European Green Capital. Ten years on, the city has been included in a list of the twenty most eco-friendly travel destinations, compiled by global travel site Big 7 Travel. Bristol is also the UK’s first Cycling City, the UK’s second Gold Sustainable Food City and a fair-trade city. With all these green accolades, it is no wonder that the city is home to a number of organisations with an environmental focus. Sustrans, a sustainable transport NGO, and the Soil Association, a charity working to revolutionise farming, eating practices, and the use of natural resources, are just two examples.

GREEN SPACES AND PATHS

So what is it that makes Bristol a great place to visit for the environmentally-conscious? Aside from green spaces in the form of over four hundred parks and gardens, Bristol also boasts city farms and thirty nature reserves in the city and surrounding area, including salt marshes and wildflower grasslands. Being a city that prioritises sustainable travel, Bristol is easily accessible to those on foot or travelling by bike or e-scooter. Many visitors and locals enjoy the Bristol to Bath Railway Path, a 13-mile (20.9-kilometre) route that is available to cyclists, walkers and disabled users alike. Places

PLANT-BASED

Bristol is also a haven for those looking for sustainable food, drink and shopping options. Named the world’s vegan capital by food lovers’ website Chef’s Pencil, Bristol is a city with innumerable plant-based dining options. Restaurants such as The Canteen, a purely vegan and vegetarian restaurant, offer guests seasonal options and plant-based versions of classic British dishes, such as Vegan Wellington (in place of the traditional Beef Wellington.) And savvy shoppers will enjoy browsing second-hand clothing and other items in the city — there are around 145 charity shops and sixteen car boot sales to enjoy.

EDUCATION

For those looking to educate themselves on environmental issues while in the city, it is well worth visiting the Create Centre and Ecohome, where a variety of events and exhibitions with a focus on sustainability are offered all year round. The Ecohome displays how houses can be built with nature in mind, integrating environmentally-friendly construction methods. We The Curious, a Bristol-based science centre, is also a must-see attraction. In 2019, this interactive museum declared a climate emergency and promised to become carbon net zero by 2030. So, in short, if you’re looking to visit a green city in the UK, Bristol should be at the top of your list!
