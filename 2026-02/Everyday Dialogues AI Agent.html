Everyday Dialogues: AI Agent [B2]

L’intelligenza artificiale è sempre più presente nei luoghi di lavoro. Uno strumento che ci può dare una mano con i compiti più ripetitivi.

John: So, artificial intelligence has taken the burden off the customer service department. Sarah: Yes, the AI agent has reduced the workload a lot. John: I understand we had some initial hiccups? Sarah: Yes. It can handle standard enquiries, but anything complex or out of the ordinary can cause problems. John: What happens if it can’t resolve an issue? Sarah: Then it escalates the enquiry to a human agent. John: But it’s getting better, right? Sarah: Yes. Machine learning means it can deal with most calls now. The answers are also getting more detailed and accurate. John: What’s the resolution rate at the moment? Sarah: It’s 65 per cent, which is very good. John: Do we have any feedback from our customers? Sarah: Yes, they really like the live chat function. Average ratings are four out of five. John: Great. Given it’s success, we might roll it out to other areas of the business. Language

NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!

When you free someone or something from a lot of work, you take the burden off them. Workload is the total amount of work to be done. The word hiccups can be used to refer to small, temporary problems that are easily fixed. To handle is a synonym for ‘to manage’. Out of the ordinary means unusual. An issue is an important topic. It is often used as a synonym for ‘problem’. Machine learning refers to the use of algorithms and statistical models that allow computer systems to learn and adapt without explicit instructions. To deal with something, often a problem, means to take action to resolve it. Resolution rate is the percentage of customer support tickets or service requests that are fully resolved within a specific time period. Feedback is information about the reactions to a product or service, used as the basis for improvement. Live chat is real-time, text-based online communication between people or, in this case, between a customer and the AI agent. Ratings are a measurement of how good or popular something is. Used as a preposition, given means ‘taking into account’ or ‘considering’. To roll out is the same as ‘to introduce’ or ‘to launch’.

Glossary

  • escalates = inoltrare
  • four out of five = quattro su cinque
  • taking into account = prendere in considerazione
  • enquiries = richieste
  • accurate = precise
  • amount = quantità
  • fixed = risolvere
  • topic = questione, argomento
  • support tickets = ticket, richieste di assistenza
