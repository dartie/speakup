Global superstar Michael Jackson was [dubbed](## "soprannominare") the King of Pop in the 1980s and dominated the music world for decades, becoming one of the 20th century’s most culturally significant figures. Seventeen years after his [untimely](## "prematura") death, a new biopic aspires to maintain this acclaim, with Jackson’s [nephew](## "nipote") Jaafar Jackson in the title role.
Michael Jackson is a [polarising](## "che genera divisione") figure and has been accused of child abuse. However, this film largely focuses on the singer’s musical career: aged just six when his father created the Jackson 5 with his four brothers, the group was soon signed to the iconic Motown [record label](## "etichetta discografica") and their first four singles were [chart toppers](## "successi in cima alla classifica"). It was Jackson’s solo career, however, that catapulted him to worldwide fame, and his 1982 album “Thriller” remains the best-selling album of all time. 

## 1. I WANT YOU BACK (1969)
The Jackson 5’s debut was a huge [smash](## "successo travolgente") following a [milestone](## "pietra miliare") performance on The Ed Sullivan Show. The only single from the album “Diana Ross Presents The Jackson 5”, the title led listeners to believe the American soul singer had discovered the family quintet. The lyrics are very simple, with eleven-year-old Jackson singing about a failed relationship, using mostly the past simple.
When I had you to myself,
I didn’t want you around
Those pretty faces always made you [stand out](## "spiccare") in a [crowd](## "folla")
But someone picked you from the [bunch](## "mucchio"), one [glance](## "occhiata") was all it took
Now, it’s much too late for me to take a second look
The Jackson 5 (photographed in 1975) consisted of five brothers: Jackie, Tito, Jermaine, Marlon, and Michael.

## 2. ABC (1970)
The second single by the Jackson 5 was their second Number One and sold two million copies in its first week. A tribute to learning, the song compares love to schooling and includes the line “i before e except after c,” a classic English mnemonic for [spelling](## "scrivere") words. “Sing simple melodies, that’s how easy love can be” — and learning too!
A B C,
It’s easy as 1 2 3,
as simple as
do re mi,
A B C, 1 2 3
baby, you and me, girl

## 3. BEN (1972)
Jackson’s first solo Number One was about a boy with a pet rat. The theme for a film of the same name, it was from the thirteen-year-old’s second solo album while he continued to record with his brothers’ band. The lyrics reflect the singer’s anguish at people’s reaction to his strange pet, concluding with an example of the second conditional.
They don’t see you as I do
I wish they would try to
I’m sure they’d think again
If they had a friend like Ben

## 4. DON’T STOP TILL YOU GET ENOUGH (1979)
For his fifth solo album “Off the Wall”, Jackson was finally given creative control and this was the first song he ever wrote alone. Together with iconic producer Quincy Jones, he created this disco masterpiece which introduced his falsetto and [vocal hiccups](## "singhiozzi vocalici"). The lyrics are [peppered](## "disseminare") with imperatives but the spoken intro features the past continuous and indirect speech.
You know, I was
I was wondering, you know
If you could keep on
Because the force
It’s got a lot of power
And it make(s) me feel like, ah
It make(s) me feel like, ooh

## 5. BILLIE JEAN (1983)
The second single from “Thriller”, the world’s best-selling album of all time, was a tale of a groupie who [claimed](## "affermare") Jackson had [fathered](## "essere il padre di") her child. Producer Quincy Jones was initially against the song’s release, but the singer [presciently](## "con lungimiranza") insisted and it became a huge [smash](## "successo travolgente") hit. He introduced the world to his [signature](## "distintivo") moonwalk and single silver [rhinestone](## "strass") glove in a Motown television special, cementing his iconic status. The lyrics narrate a fictional [lawsuit](## "causa legale") using the past simple and reported speech. 
She told me her name was Billie Jean
as she caused a scene
Then every head turned with eyes
that dreamed of bein’ the one
Who will dance on the floor in the round

## 6. THRILLER (1983)
The album’s title track was its record seventh single, all US Top 10 hits (including the Number Ones Billie Jean and [Beat It](## "vattene")). A horror story in the present simple with a lexical set of nocturnal [threats](## "minacce"), this disco-funk track was accompanied by a thirteen-minute short film with Jackson transforming into a [werewolf](## "licantropo") and dancing with [ghouls](## "spettri") in a [graveyard](## "cimitero"). The song scarily concludes with the sonorous voice of legendary horror actor Vincent Price and his [manic laughter](## "risata folle").
It’s close to midnight
Something evil’s [lurking](## "nascondersi") in the dark
Under the moonlight
You see a sight that almost stops your heart
The 1985 We Are the World video featuring Paul Simon, Kim Carnes, Michael Jackson, and Diana Ross, among others.

## 7. WE ARE THE WORLD (1985)
Noting the success of the UK’s Band Aid charity single Do They Know It’s Christmas, with all [proceeds](## "ricavati") going to Ethiopian [famine relief](## "aiuti contro la carestia"), Jackson and Lionel Richie wrote this simple [reminder](## "promemoria") of equality and unity. Produced by Quincy Jones, it was recorded in just one night after an awards ceremony and features the crème of the country’s singers under the [moniker](## "nome, pseudonimo") USA for Africa. It is the best-selling single of the last sixty years and was the first time Jackson publicly worked on a humanitarian project.
There comes a time
When we [heed](## "fare attenzione") a certain call
When the world must
come together as one
There are people dying
Oh, and it’s time [to lend a hand](## "dare una mano") to life
The greatest gift of all

## 8. MAN IN THE MIRROR (1987)
Following the success of “Thriller”, Jackson embarked on the Victory Tour, his last one with his brothers, the Jacksons. On his return he reunited with Quincy Jones to work on his seventh solo album “Bad”, which [spawned](## "generare") a record five US Number Ones. Man in the Mirror is a reflective call to make the world a better place, using the slang ‘gonna’ (‘going to’) for intentions.
I’m gonna make a change
For once in my life
It’s gonna feel real good
Gonna make a difference
gonna make it right
The official poster for the musical biopic Michael.

## 9. HEAL THE WORLD (1991)
For his next album, “Dangerous”, Jackson worked with different producers to create a [broader range](## "gamma più ampia") of styles, including electronic and hip-hop. Many of the tracks [dealt with](## "occuparsi di") social issues, but this linguistically low-level ballad is the song he [claimed](## "affermare") to be most proud to have created. In 1992, he set up the [Heal](## "guarire") the World Foundation, saying he wanted “to improve the conditions for children throughout the world.” 
[Heal](## "guarire") the world
Make it a better place
For you and for me
And the entire human race
There are people dying
If you care enough for the living
Make a better place
For you and for me

## 10. THIS IS IT (2009)
Over the years, Jackson had become the subject of intense media speculation. Eccentricities included unusual pets, such as a snake and a chimp named Bubbles, his [purchase](## "acquisto") of the Beatles’ music rights, and his changing skin colour. His marriage to Elvis Presley’s daughter, and his three children, also [came under scrutiny](## "finire sotto esame"). In 1993 he was first accused of child sexual abuse at his Neverland ranch, and he became a recluse, spending much time living abroad. When he announced a 2009 fifty-date residency in London, ticket-selling sites [went into a frenzy](## "andare in delirio"). He died of an overdose eighteen days before the first date, [sending shockwaves](## "scatenare sconcerto, scalpore") around the world. A demo of this song was found during preparations for a film documentary, and Jackson’s brothers helped complete the recording. It features the [Irishism](## "espressione tipicamente irlandese") ‘grand’, meaning ‘okay’, perhaps reflecting the six months he lived in the country in 2006.
This is it, here I stand
I’m the light of the world, I feel grand
And this love, I can feel
And I know, yes, for sure, it is real
