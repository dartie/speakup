According to a 2012 [poll](## "sondaggio"), the [Sunday roast](## "arrosto della domenica") is the second thing Brits love the most about their country (the first is bacon sandwiches!) This is [hardly](## "appena") surprising, given their love of nice [meals](## "pasti") and tradition. A more modest version of the Christmas dinner, the [Sunday roast](## "arrosto della domenica") is the perfect excuse to [indulge](## "concedersi") in some [yummy](## "gustoso") food with family or friends, at home or in the pub.

## BEEFEATING NATION
The most [widespread](## "diffusa") version of the origins of the [Sunday roast](## "arrosto della domenica") takes us back to the 15th century. It is believed that the royal guards of King Henry VII would enjoy a little [feast](## "banchetto") of roasted [beef](## "manzo") every Sunday after church. This is why they came to be known as ‘[beef](## "manzo")eaters’. In the following centuries, the tradition spread to all the [households](## "case") that could [afford](## "permettersi") it, and it became a Sunday [staple](## "elemento fondamentale"). Eating a large meal after the Sunday service was common all over Europe, and the roast variant prevailed in the British Isles.

## GOT A SPARE OVEN?
Traditionally, families prepared the meal before the Sunday service. The meat would be left to roast for hours, and it would be ready by the time they went back home after [mass](## "messa"). Then, vegetables such as potatoes and [turnips](## "rape") would be added. The juices from the meat and vegetables would be used to make a [thick](## "densa") [gravy](## "salsa fatta col fondo di cottura della carne") to [pour](## "versare") over them. Not all [households](## "case") owned a [fireplace](## "camino") to make their Sunday dinner, however, so some people took their ingredients to a local [bakery](## "panetteria") to roast them there, while they attended church. Afterwards, they would pick their food up and take it home, just like an early version of a takeaway! Since it was a big meal, [leftovers](## "avanzi") would be used to make [stews](## "stufati") and [pies](## "pasticci") in the following days.

## THE MODERN SUNDAY ROAST 
Although meat is made in the [oven](## "forno") nowadays and not roasted by the fire, the [Sunday roast](## "arrosto della domenica") remains a powerful tradition. Many UK pubs offer it even on weekdays! 
The modern [Sunday roast](## "arrosto della domenica") consists of a large piece of meat — it’s often roast [beef](## "manzo"), but it can also be [lamb](## "agnello"), pork, or chicken —, with potatoes and vegetables, and of course some delicious [gravy](## "salsa fatta col fondo di cottura della carne"). The most common [side dishes](## "contorni") for the roast include Yorkshire puddings, sausages, and a variety of [sauces](## "salse"), like apple or [cranberry](## "mirtillo rosso") sauce.
