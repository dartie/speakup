Some film sequels take longer than others to [grace](## "onorare") our screens. Twenty years after the release of the hit movie The Devil Wears Prada, its sequel is [set to be released](## "annunciato in uscita") this month. The original film, based on Lauren Weisberger’s 2003 best-selling novel of the same name, was a [resounding](## "clamoroso") box office success, [grossing](## "incassare") over $326 million. 
The film was extremely [quotable](## "ricco di frasi memorabili ('to quote' = citare)") and led to the creation of many viral memes, but it’s not surprising fans need a [memory jog](## "aiutino per la memoria") — it’s been almost two decades. That’s why many people are revisiting the original film. In August 2025, just after the first images of the sequel appeared on social media, The Devil Wears Prada shot to number four among the most-watched titles on Disney+ platform worldwide. 

## BASED ON ACTUAL PEOPLE
[In a nutshell](## "in sintesi"), the first film [revolves around](## "ruotare attorno a") the toxic workplace environment that Andy, a young assistant played by Anne Hathaway, finds herself in when she gets a job working for Miranda Priestly (played by Meryl Streep), the [editor-in-chief](## "caporedattrice") of [Runway](## "passerella"), a fictional fashion publication based in New York City. Interestingly, Weisberger previously worked as a personal assistant to the renowned [editor-in-chief](## "caporedattrice") of Vogue, Anna Wintour, which [fuelled](## "alimentare") the widespread rumour that Wintour directly inspired the character of Miranda Priestly. 
Meryl Streep and Stanley Tucci, who plays art director Nigel Kipling.

## PRODUCTION PROBLEMS
This led to issues during the production of the first film, as designers, fashion models, and Hollywood agents did not want to burn their bridges with Wintour. “I had enormous trouble finding anyone in the fashion world who’d talk to me, because people were afraid of Anna and Vogue, not wanting to be blackballed,” Aline Brosh McKenna, who was hired to write a new screenplay for the original movie in 2004, told Entertainment Weekly.

## MODERN MEDIA
While Weisberger did write a sequel to her original 2003 novel ten years later, Revenge Wears Prada: The Devil Returns, the plot has been adapted to reflect the modern media landscape, which is a very different beast to the one found in 2013: The Devil Wears Prada 2 sees Andy returning to work at [Runway](## "passerella"), where Miranda is dealing with the challenges of declining [advertising revenue](## "ricavi pubblicitari") for [print publications](## "pubblicazioni cartacee"). Changing norms in the industry threaten to [shake up](## "sconvolgere") the status quo. One of Miranda’s previous assistants, Emily (played by Emily Blunt), could be the key to the survival of [Runway](## "passerella"), as she is now the head of a luxury brand with available funding for advertising.

## NOSTALGIA
The success of this sequel [relies heavily on](## "dipendere in gran misura da") nostalgia, but it is questionable how the premise of a toxic workplace led by a brutal boss will [resonate with](## "trovare riscontro in") Gen Z viewers today. What once seemed funny could easily [raise eyebrows](## "alzare le sopracciglia, destare stupore") today, so the film would benefit from a tongue-in-cheek  approach.
