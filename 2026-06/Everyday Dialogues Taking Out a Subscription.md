Luca: Hi, I’m calling to enquire about the subscription to Speak Up.
Laura: Good morning! Thanks for your interest. Are you familiar with the magazine?
Luca: Yes, I’ve seen it on the newsstands and my language school uses it for listening exercises.
Laura: Okay, so, you know that the articles have audio, exercises and a glossary.
Luca: Yes, it’s great that you can listen and read at the same time.
Laura: That’s good to hear. Are you interested in the digital or print edition?
Luca: I guess I like reading a physical magazine.
Laura: Okay, well, a print subscription also includes access to the digital edition via the web or our app, where you can access the magazine’s archive.
Luca: What’s that?
Laura: It means you can read past issues going [all the way back to](## "fino a") 2022, plus lots of exclusive content, such as grammar lessons.
Luca: That sounds great. How much is that?
Laura: Right now it’s €38,97 per trimester, but if you subscribe via the web, you get a 62 per cent discount. That works out at €15 for three issues.
Luca: Oh, I’ll do that! Please give me the link.

## NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!
"I’m calling to enquire about..." is a formal, [polite](## "educata") phrase used to ask for information.
When you are familiar with something, you know about it.
A newsstand is an [outdoor](## "outdoor") temporary structure used as a shop for selling newspapers and magazines.
"That’s good to hear" is a commonly-used response to good news.
A print edition is a physical, paper-based copy of a publication; a digital edition is an electronic version, often in an interactive online format, with multimedia [features](## "caratteristiche").
Starting a sentence with "I guess" (or "I suppose" or "probably") indicates [uncertainty](## "incertezza") or indifference. Here, the speaker is not [fully committed](## "pienamente sicuro") to the answer and could be persuaded [otherwise](## "altrimenti").
An issue refers to a single, specific release of a magazine or newspaper, published on a particular date, [i.e.](## "cioè ('id est')"), the June issue of Speak Up. 
"To work out" in this context means to calculate an [amount](## "quantità").
