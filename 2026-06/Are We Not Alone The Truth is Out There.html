Are We Not Alone? The Truth is Out There [B2]

Al di là di fantasie intergalattiche e teorie del complotto, il dibattito sull’esistenza di forme di vita intelligenti oltre il nostro pianeta diventa sempre più rilevante con l’avanzare della tecnologia e la rivelazione di segreti militari.

For many people, this could be the turning point in human history: the moment when the existence of alien life on the universe is finally confirmed. Known as ‘Disclosure’, this hypothetical revelation is at the centre of a global movement, which demands that the government — or the United States Government more specifically — divulge classified information about aliens and UFOs; something President Donald Trump has promised to do. It is also the premise of a new film by Steven Spielberg titled Disclosure Day, about what happens when humanity receives definitive proof that aliens exist.

MODERN CONSPIRACIES

Modern speculation about the existence of aliens and a government alien cover-up began with the so-called Roswell UFO incident of 1947. In July of that year, a flying object crashed on a ranch near Roswell in the US state of New Mexico. The US Army initially said it had recovered a “flying disc” from the crash site, before retracting this claim. However, many people believe the initial claim and that the government subsequently lied about their recovery of the flying disc, or unidentified flying object (UFO), as well as alien bodies. A scientific study of UFOs dating to the 1960s describes how supposed sightings can be of explainable phenomena, such as streetlamp reflections, lenticular or noctilucent clouds, mirages, or parhelia phenomena (atmospheric optical illusions consisting of bright patches of light that appear around the sun).

ROSWELL

The Roswell UFO incident inspired modern alien conspiracies, which reemerged in the public narrative in 2017, after a group of former Pentagon and government officials leaked three US Navy videos of unknown objects to the media. The first was filmed by a pilot flying an F/A18F Super Hornet operating from the aircraft carrier USS Nimitz in 2004. It shows a white, oblong object hovering and moving rapidly over the Pacific Ocean approximately one hundred miles southwest of San Diego, California. The other two were filmed in 2015 from aircraft conducting training exercises off the east coast of Florida, and show objects moving rapidly in the sky while pilots express their incredulity at what they are seeing.

OFFICIAL INVESTIGATIONS

Perhaps in response to public pressure for more transparency regarding UFOs, the US government made some important announcements in 2022. First, the Pentagon officially declassified and confirmed the authenticity of the three leaked videos; however, the government continues to investigate the objects captured in the videos, which remain unidentified to this day. Also that year, the US Department of Defense established the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) to investigate Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAPs), the preferred official term for UFOs. And NASA convened an independent study team to examine UAPs from a scientific perspective. Current Affairs

EARLIER SIGHTINGS

This wasn’t the first time the US government had investigated alien phenomena. Between 1947 and 1969, the US Air Force had investigated more than twelve thousand UFO sightings; it concluded that most of them involved artificial objects or atmospheric phenomena, while 701 remained unexplained. The investigations by the AARO and NASA team, whose findings were published in 2023 and 2024, drew similar conclusions. They found no proof of alien life or of a government alien cover-up, and found that most UAPs were objects such as balloons, drones, birds or atmospheric phenomena.

THE AGE OF DISCLOSURE

Despite these official government findings, the speculation about the existence of aliens continues. It was further fuelled by a documentary released last year titled The Age of Disclosure. The documentary features interviews with former and current employees of the US government, military, and intelligence community, as well as ufologists and fringe scientists. They make some extraordinary claims, including that the US government not only knows about the existence of aliens but that it has been retrieving their crafts and bodies for decades and attempting to reverse engineer their advanced technology. Naturally, the documentary received a strong response from both sceptics and believers, with many pointing out the lack of concrete proof for the claims made in it. PRESIDENTIAL INPUT

The public discourse about aliens and UFOs came to the forefront again this year, following comments from former US president Barack Obama, and a response from Donald Trump. In February, in response to the question “Are aliens real?” on the podcast No Lie with Brian Tyler Cohen, Obama said, “They’re real but I haven’t seen them. And they’re not being kept in… what is it? Area 51. There’s no underground facility unless there’s this enormous conspiracy and they hid it from the President of the United States.”

LIFE OUT THERE

Obama’s comments got so much media attention that he later clarified, in an Instagram post, “Statistically, the universe is so vast that the odds are good there’s life out there. But the distances between solar systems are so great that the chances we’ve been visited by aliens is low, and I saw no evidence during my presidency that extraterrestrials have made contact with us. Really!” In response, President Trump accused Obama of disclosing classified information, and then announced that he would order federal agencies to begin releasing government files related to aliens and UFOs because of “tremendous interest” from the public.
