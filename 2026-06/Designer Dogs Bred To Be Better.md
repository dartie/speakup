You’ve heard of designer clothes, shoes or bags. But how about designer dogs? Also known as ‘dog [crossbreeds](## "incroci")’, designer dogs are not like typical [mutts](## "meticci"), which have unknown mixed [ancestry](## "ascendenza"). (That’s okay, we love them anyway!) They are intentionally [crossbred](## "incrociare") from [purebred](## "di razza, purosangue") parents, with the objective of creating a [puppy](## "cucciolo") that inherits the best [traits](## "caratteristiche") of both parents. These [custom-made](## "su misura") designer dogs are often more expensive than [purebred](## "di razza, purosangue") dogs — sometimes selling for $5,000 or more! — and have become more popular than ever in recent years.

## FROM WOLVES TO DOGS
Dog breeding is not a recent activity. It began over 15,000 years ago, with the domestication of wolves. For thousands of years, we bred dogs for purposes such as [herding](## "pascolare") and [hunting](## "cacciare"). It wasn’t until the 1800s that we began breeding dogs for physical aesthetics and uniformity, creating the standardised [purebred](## "di razza, purosangue") dogs that we know today. Then, in the late 1980s, people began intentionally crossbreeding [purebred](## "di razza, purosangue") dogs to create [so-called](## "cosiddetti") designer dogs.
A pomsky (Siberian husky and Pomeranian).

## SPECIFIC TRAITS
Designer dogs are often bred to have a specific trait, such as appearance, intelligence, or temperament. They might be bred to [shed](## "perdere pelo") less [fur](## "pelliccia, pelo") than other breeds, making them ideal pets for people with dog allergies. Or they might be bred to reduce their predisposition to genetic disorders. This is based on the assumption that crossbreeding reduces the probability of inheriting breed-specific health conditions. Popular designer dogs include the labradoodle, a cross between a Labrador retriever and a poodle; the goldendoodle (golden retriever and poodle); the cockapoo (cocker spaniel and poodle); the cavapoo (cavalier King Charles spaniel and poodle); and the pomsky (Pomeranian and Siberian husky).

## UNPREDICTABLE 
Creating a designer dog is extremely difficult because it’s impossible to predict the result. There is no guarantee that the dog will inherit the desired [traits](## "caratteristiche") from either or both parents, and have the intended appearance, intelligence, temperament, genetic health, and so on. Instead of inheriting the ‘best’ of both parents, the [puppy](## "cucciolo") can inherit the ‘worst’ of both parents. Also, their popularity and high price have contributed to the proliferation of industrial-scale [puppy](## "cucciolo") [mills](## "allevamenti intensivi"), which prioritise high-volume production over the health and happiness of the puppies. Perhaps we should [stick to](## "continuare con") our beloved [mutts](## "meticci") instead.
