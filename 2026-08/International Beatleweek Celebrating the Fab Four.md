# International Beatleweek: Celebrating the Fab Four   [B2]

Ogni agosto, migliaia di fan dei Beatles provenienti da tutto il mondo si riuniscono a Liverpool per celebrare la musica e la storia dei “Fab Four”. E il turismo locale ci guadagna!

The city of Liverpool attracts around sixty million visitors a year. As it is the hometown of The Beatles, many come to visit the iconic [venues](## "spazi, luoghi") related to the [Fab](## "favolosi") Four: the Cavern Club, where they played during their early days; the childhood homes of John Lennon and Paul McCartney; the immersive Beatles Story exhibition on the Royal Albert Dock; and, of course, Penny [Lane](## "strada, via"), made famous in the eponymous hit song. Every year in August, some 15,000 fans from over forty countries [flood](## "inondare, affluire") the city during International Beatleweek. The seven-day festival is [timed](## "programmare") to coincide with the [bank holiday](## "giorno festivo ufficiale") weekend of the last Monday in August.

## CAN’T BUY ME LOVE
International Beatleweek is recognised as the crown jewel of Liverpool’s tourism calendar. Research shows that Beatleweek visitors typically stay the whole week and spend significantly more per day on accommodation, transport and restaurants than domestic travellers. Overall, Beatles-related tourism is a huge [economic driver](## "motore economico") for Liverpool, generating up to £120 million a year in [revenue](## "entrate"). It also provides thousands of jobs and [boosts](## "dare impulso") the city’s reputation as a Unesco City of Music.
A Beatles tribute band at the Cavern Club, Liverpool.

## COME TOGETHER
International Beatleweek [features](## "presentare, includere") over two hundred performances and non-stop live music on multiple stages. The festival includes concerts, special events, exhibitions, talks and [sightseeing](## "visite turistiche") tours. The [cornerstone](## "pietra angolare, cardine") of the event is the Annual Beatles Convention Day, [held](## "svolgere, avere luogo") at the Adelphi Hotel, which once a year transforms into the home of Beatles [fandom](## "comunità di fan"). Visitors can enjoy concerts, listen to [guest speakers](## "relatori ospiti"), view [screenings](## "proiezioni"), browse [memorabilia](## "cimeli, oggetti da collezione") or participate in Ringo Bingo.

Other key [venues](## "spazi, luoghi") include the Liverpool Philharmonic Hall, Port Sunlight Village and Grand Central Hall. Visitors can also join Beatles [sightseeing](## "visite turistiche") tours to famous places such as Penny [Lane](## "strada, via") and Strawberry Field.

## ALL YOU NEED IS LOVE
International Beatleweek is more than just a music festival, it is also a meeting place for fans who want to celebrate friendship, music and history. The music of The Beatles is more than sixty years old, but their [legacy](## "eredità, lascito") continues to inspire new generations of fans. The event gives them a chance to connect with others who share their love for the [Fab](## "favolosi") Four — and experience the spirit of the band in the place where their story began.
