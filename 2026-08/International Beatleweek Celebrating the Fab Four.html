International Beatleweek: Celebrating the Fab Four [B2]

Ogni agosto, migliaia di fan dei Beatles provenienti da tutto il mondo si riuniscono a Liverpool per celebrare la musica e la storia dei “Fab Four”. E il turismo locale ci guadagna!

The city of Liverpool attracts around sixty million visitors a year. As it is the hometown of The Beatles, many come to visit the iconic venues related to the Fab Four: the Cavern Club, where they played during their early days; the childhood homes of John Lennon and Paul McCartney; the immersive Beatles Story exhibition on the Royal Albert Dock; and, of course, Penny Lane, made famous in the eponymous hit song. Every year in August, some 15,000 fans from over forty countries flood the city during International Beatleweek. The seven-day festival is timed to coincide with the bank holiday weekend of the last Monday in August.

CAN’T BUY ME LOVE

International Beatleweek is recognised as the crown jewel of Liverpool’s tourism calendar. Research shows that Beatleweek visitors typically stay the whole week and spend significantly more per day on accommodation, transport and restaurants than domestic travellers. Overall, Beatles-related tourism is a huge economic driver for Liverpool, generating up to £120 million a year in revenue. It also provides thousands of jobs and boosts the city’s reputation as a Unesco City of Music. A Beatles tribute band at the Cavern Club, Liverpool.

COME TOGETHER

International Beatleweek features over two hundred performances and non-stop live music on multiple stages. The festival includes concerts, special events, exhibitions, talks and sightseeing tours. The cornerstone of the event is the Annual Beatles Convention Day, held at the Adelphi Hotel, which once a year transforms into the home of Beatles fandom. Visitors can enjoy concerts, listen to guest speakers, view screenings, browse memorabilia or participate in Ringo Bingo. Entertainment

ALL YOU NEED IS LOVE

International Beatleweek is more than just a music festival, it is also a meeting place for fans who want to celebrate friendship, music and history. The music of The Beatles is more than sixty years old, but their legacy continues to inspire new generations of fans. The event gives them a chance to connect with others who share their love for the Fab Four — and experience the spirit of the band in the place where their story began.
