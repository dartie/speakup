Everyday Dialogues: Dealing with an Abusive Boss [B1]

Avere a che fare con il proprio capo, in alcuni casi, può risultare alquanto complicato. Ecco come affrontare una conversazione con un superiore che non si comporta in modo professionale.

Helen: Excuse me, sir. May I talk to you about something important? Boss: Make it quick. I’m very busy.  Helen: About our meeting earlier, I just want to say that I feel uncomfortable when I’m shouted at in front of my colleagues. It affects my confidence and my work. Boss: Well, if you did your job properly, I wouldn’t need to shout. Helen: Look, I know I messed up, but perhaps you could give your feedback in a more respectful way. Boss: Are you saying that I am the problem? Helen: No, sir. I’m just trying to find a solution so we can work better together. Boss: What kind of solution? Helen: Maybe focus on constructive criticism? If I make a mistake, you could explain what went wrong and how to correct it. That would help me improve, going forward. Boss: Okay, go on. Helen: From my side, I know I get defensive and start arguing. I’ll make every effort to listen calmly and to ask questions. The main thing is to avoid conflict. Boss: That sounds reasonable. I’ll try to communicate more clearly. Helen: Thank you, sir. I’m sure the whole team will get better results. Boss: Okay. Just don’t mess up again. Language

NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!

The idiom “make it quick” means to do something rapidly, hurry up or be brief. Starting a statement with “I just want to say that” makes it sound milder and softens the impact. Using the passive voice of “when I’m shouted at” reduces blame, compared to the direct approach when you shout at me, which is more confrontational. ‘To mess up’ means ‘to make a mistake’. ‘Feedback’ refers to opinions or reactions given to someone about their performance. ‘Going forward’ means ‘from now on’ or ‘in the future’. ‘Go on’ means ‘continue’. “To make every effort” implies a strong commitment to achieving a goal. Alternatives include “to try as hard as possible” or “to do everything within your power”. “The main thing” means “the most important issue”.

Glossary

  • commitment = impegno
  • busy = occupato
  • confidence = fiducia in se stessi, sicurezza
  • properly = come si deve, correttamente
  • improve = migliorare
  • avoid = evitare
  • statement = affermazione
  • blame = colpa
  • approach = approccio
  • arguing = discutere
  • milder = più moderato
  • softens = attenuare, ammorbidire
