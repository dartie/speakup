Helen: Excuse me, sir. May I talk to you about something important?
Boss: Make it quick. I’m very [busy](## "occupato").
 Helen: About our meeting earlier, I just want to say that I feel uncomfortable when I’m shouted at in front of my colleagues. It affects my [confidence](## "fiducia in se stessi, sicurezza") and my work.
Boss: Well, if you did your job [properly](## "come si deve, correttamente"), I wouldn’t need to shout.
Helen: Look, I know I messed up, but perhaps you could give your feedback in a more respectful way.
Boss: Are you saying that I am the problem?
Helen: No, sir. I’m just trying to find a solution so we can work better together.
Boss: What kind of solution?
Helen: Maybe focus on constructive criticism? If I make a mistake, you could explain what went wrong and how to correct it. That would help me [improve](## "migliorare"), going forward.
Boss: Okay, go on.
Helen: From my side, I know I get defensive and start [arguing](## "discutere"). I’ll make every effort to listen calmly and to ask questions. The main thing is to [avoid](## "evitare") conflict.
Boss: That sounds reasonable. I’ll try to communicate more clearly.
Helen: Thank you, sir. I’m sure the whole team will get better results.
Boss: Okay. Just don’t mess up again.

## NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!
The idiom "make it quick" means to do something rapidly, hurry up or be brief.
Starting a [statement](## "affermazione") with "I just want to say that" makes it sound [milder](## "più moderato") and [softens](## "attenuare, ammorbidire") the impact.
Using the passive voice of "when I’m shouted at" reduces [blame](## "colpa"), compared to the direct [approach](## "approccio") when you shout at me, which is more confrontational.
'To mess up' means 'to make a mistake'.
'Feedback' refers to opinions or reactions given to someone about their performance.
'Going forward' means 'from now on' or 'in the future'.
'Go on' means 'continue'.
"To make every effort" implies a strong [commitment](## "impegno") to achieving a goal. Alternatives include "to try as hard as possible" or "to do everything within your power".
"The main thing" means "the most important issue".

* **arguing** = discutere
* **milder** = più moderato
* **softens** = attenuare, ammorbidire
