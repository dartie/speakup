How to Pour the Perfect Pint of Guinness [C1]

Sapevi che per ottenere una Guinness perfetta esiste un metodo di spillatura che richiede la massima precisione? Ecco tutti i passaggi da seguire.

Pouring a perfect pint of Guinness follows a special ritual known as the ‘two-part pour’. First, use a Guinness pint glass and hold it at a 45° angle under the beer tap. Pour the Guinness slowly as the liquid rises, then gradually straighten the glass upright until it reaches the harp logo on the glass (about three-quarters full.) Then place the glass on the bar and leave it to rest for 119.5 seconds. During this time, the famous surge and settle occurs: tiny bubbles move down the sides and rise through the middle, creating a dark body and a creamy white head. This method matters because Guinness uses a blend of nitrogen and CO₂ rather than CO₂ alone as most beers do, which produces smaller, finer bubbles. The pause allows these bubbles to settle properly, giving the drink its characteristic thick, velvety texture. Culture
