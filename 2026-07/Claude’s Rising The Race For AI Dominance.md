Until recently, Chat[GPT](## "trasformatore generativo preaddestrato ('generative pre-trained transformer')") was the undisputed leader in AI, the [go-to](## "di riferimento") tool for quick answers and creative solutions. For nearly three years it dominated the market, building a strong user base among [developers](## "sviluppatori"), professionals, and casual users. But in 2026, the unthinkable happened: a rival chatbot named Claude began to seriously challenge its position, even surpassing Chat[GPT](## "trasformatore generativo preaddestrato ('generative pre-trained transformer')") in app downloads and reaching the top of app store ratings in several countries.

## THE AI BUSINESS
Claude’s [overtaking](## "superare") of the market follows increased scrutiny over AI ethics, which include US government partnerships. Launched in November 2022 by OpenAI, Chat[GPT](## "trasformatore generativo preaddestrato ('generative pre-trained transformer')") brought generative AI to the masses, powered by large language models (LLMs) trained on vast datasets. Anthropic, the company that developed Claude, [set itself apart](## "distinguersi") with a stronger emphasis on safety and ethical alignment. Claude has also been favoured for being more technologically advanced.

## QUIT CHAT[GPT](## "trasformatore generativo preaddestrato ('generative pre-trained transformer')")
OpenAI, Anthropic and other AI companies rely on human feedback. However, Anthropic [cross-checks](## "verificare, confrontare") its responses against a set of written principles, revising them when necessary. This ethical [stance](## "posizione") was highlighted in February 2026 when Anthropic refused to sign a controversial deal with the US Department of Defense, citing concerns over the [mass surveillance](## "sorveglianza di massa") of American citizens. OpenAI’s signing of the deal, which would allow military use of its models, [sparked](## "innescare") millions of people to join a ‘Quit[GPT](## "trasformatore generativo preaddestrato ('generative pre-trained transformer')")’ movement, which sent Claude to number one in the App store. Dario Amodei, Anthropic’s [CEO](## "amministratore delegato ('chief executive officer')"), was [formerly](## "precedentemente") Vice President of Research at OpenAI but left around 2020-2021 over strategic and ethical differences.

## TECHNICAL SUPERIORITY
Claude has also been winning over technical users, especially [developers](## "sviluppatori"). Claude Code, Anthropic’s [flagship](## "fiore all’occhiello") coding assistant, now dominates the AI programming market, generating over $2.5 billion annually, making it one of the fastest-growing AI coding tools. Why? Because it excels in real-world tasks. While other tools struggle with large or [outdated](## "obsoleti") codebases, Claude Code can navigate millions of lines of code, see how different parts [fit together](## "incastrarsi"), and make intelligent changes without breaking existing systems. For companies, this is changing the way software is made and updated.

## IT’S ALL IN THE IMAGE
Another key factor is tone. Chat[GPT](## "trasformatore generativo preaddestrato ('generative pre-trained transformer')") has often been criticised for its [overly- agreeable](## "eccessivamente accomodanti"), sometimes [saccharine](## "sdolcinate") responses. Claude, on the other hand, offers a more balanced and precise interaction, appealing to users who value [reliability](## "affidabilità") and depth. Still, researchers are pointing out that it’s premature to talk about an exodus, with Chat[GPT](## "trasformatore generativo preaddestrato ('generative pre-trained transformer')") still claiming 900 million active weekly users worldwide. The impact is for the moment largely reputational, but in an age where image matters greatly, this may well [reverberate](## "avere ripercussioni") faster than expected.
