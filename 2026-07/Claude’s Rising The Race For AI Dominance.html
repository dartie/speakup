Claude’s Rising: The Race For AI Dominance [B2]

È già giunta la fine per ChatGPT? L’ascesa di nuovi strumenti di intelligenza artificiale, come Claude di Anthropic, sta mettendo in crisi il dominio di OpenAI.

Until recently, ChatGPT was the undisputed leader in AI, the go-to tool for quick answers and creative solutions. For nearly three years it dominated the market, building a strong user base among developers, professionals, and casual users. But in 2026, the unthinkable happened: a rival chatbot named Claude began to seriously challenge its position, even surpassing ChatGPT in app downloads and reaching the top of app store ratings in several countries.

THE AI BUSINESS

Claude’s overtaking of the market follows increased scrutiny over AI ethics, which include US government partnerships. Launched in November 2022 by OpenAI, ChatGPT brought generative AI to the masses, powered by large language models (LLMs) trained on vast datasets. Anthropic, the company that developed Claude, set itself apart with a stronger emphasis on safety and ethical alignment. Claude has also been favoured for being more technologically advanced. QUIT CHATGPT

OpenAI, Anthropic and other AI companies rely on human feedback. However, Anthropic cross-checks its responses against a set of written principles, revising them when necessary. This ethical stance was highlighted in February 2026 when Anthropic refused to sign a controversial deal with the US Department of Defense, citing concerns over the mass surveillance of American citizens. OpenAI’s signing of the deal, which would allow military use of its models, sparked millions of people to join a ‘QuitGPT’ movement, which sent Claude to number one in the App store. Dario Amodei, Anthropic’s CEO, was formerly Vice President of Research at OpenAI but left around 2020-2021 over strategic and ethical differences.

TECHNICAL SUPERIORITY

Claude has also been winning over technical users, especially developers. Claude Code, Anthropic’s flagship coding assistant, now dominates the AI programming market, generating over $2.5 billion annually, making it one of the fastest-growing AI coding tools. Why? Because it excels in real-world tasks. While other tools struggle with large or outdated codebases, Claude Code can navigate millions of lines of code, see how different parts fit together, and make intelligent changes without breaking existing systems. For companies, this is changing the way software is made and updated.

IT’S ALL IN THE IMAGE

Another key factor is tone. ChatGPT has often been criticised for its overly- agreeable, sometimes saccharine responses. Claude, on the other hand, offers a more balanced and precise interaction, appealing to users who value reliability and depth. Still, researchers are pointing out that it’s premature to talk about an exodus, with ChatGPT still claiming 900 million active weekly users worldwide. The impact is for the moment largely reputational, but in an age where image matters greatly, this may well reverberate faster than expected.
