Everyday Dialogues: Dealing with a Complaint [B1]

Un reclamo può essere gestito attraverso una conversazione educata ma molto efficace. Con calma, cortesia e le espressioni giuste si può risolvere un problema senza stress.

James: Excuse me, I’m not happy with this product. It stopped working after a couple of days. Laura: I’m really sorry to hear that. Let me see what I can do. James: I paid quite a lot for it, so this is pretty frustrating. Laura: I completely understand where you’re coming from. Do you happen to have the receipt with you? James: Yes, I do. Here it is. Laura: Alright, no problem at all. We can sort this out for you. We can offer you a replacement or give you a full refund. James: To be honest, I’d rather get a refund. Laura: That’s absolutely fine. I’ll take care of it right away. Could you hand over your credit card? I’ll have the full amount charged back to your account. James: Alright. I appreciate it. Laura: There. All done. James: Great! Thanks for your help. Laura: No problem at all. Sorry for the inconvenience. Language

NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!

‘Pretty’ is the same as quite or rather. “I understand where you’re coming from” means that you recognise and accept someone’s point of view. “Do you happen to…?” is a polite and indirect way to start a question. ‘To sort out’ means to resolve a problem. “To be honest” (or TBH in texts) is a common phrase used to introduce a sincere but potentially unwelcome opinion. “I’d rather…” is used to express a preference in a polite way. When you ‘fix a problem’ or ‘deal with a situation’, you ‘take care of it’. ‘Right away’ means immediately, without delay. ‘To hand something over’ means to give an object to another person. ‘To charge back’ refers to the reversal of a credit card transaction, to return money to the card holder’s bank account. “I appreciate it” is a polite expression of gratitude. When you say, “All done”, it means the issue has been completely resolved. “Sorry for the inconvenience” is a polite way of apologising for any trouble that has been caused.

Glossary

  • replacement = sostituzione
  • full refund = rimborso completo
  • amount = somma
  • unwelcome = indesiderata
  • quite = abbastanza
  • rather = piuttosto
  • polite = gentile
  • delay = ritardo
  • card holder = titolare della carta
