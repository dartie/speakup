Gen Alpha is [straight up](## "veramente, proprio") the first [squad](## "squadra, gruppo") [raised](## "crescere") fully online — like, no before wi-fi memories at all. These kids are [low-key](## "discreti") built different, growing up with AI, tablets and endless content [on lock](## "sotto controllo, in pugno"). Raised by Millennials, their vibe mixes [tech smarts](## "abilità tecnologiche") with new-school values and big [awareness](## "consapevolezza") energy. If you wanna get the future [fr](## "davvero ('for real')"), you gotta understand Gen Alpha — their [mindset](## "mentalità"), their [slang](## "gergo") and how they’re about to change the game.
Did you understand any of that? If not, don’t worry. You were probably born before 2010. It’s just an example of the way young people might talk, if they are part of the [cohort](## "gruppo, generazione") known as Generation Alpha.
The above paragraph basically explains that this group was born in a digital world with smartphones, [devices](## "dispositivi") and constant connectivity. The [tech-savvy](## "esperti di tecnologia") children of Millennials (born between 1981 and 1996), they have adopted their parents’ social values. Understanding them will help to predict their future impact on the world.

## NEW KIDS ON THE BLOCK
Generation Alpha was born between 2010 and 2025. The oldest are just finishing secondary education, while the youngest are still at pre-school age. They lived through the Covid pandemic and many of them experienced social distancing and remote schooling.
Growing up with technology has given these ‘screenagers’ a high digital [literacy](## "competenza"), as well as a need for [fast-paced](## "rapidi"), visual learning environments. They are exposed to a [relentless](## "incessante") [stream](## "flusso") of information, delivered via the internet to their [devices](## "dispositivi"), and this is shaping their perspective on life. It is no surprise that Gen Alpha [whole-heartedly](## "completamente") embraces artificial intelligence. Fortunately, they are better at detecting what is real and what is AI-generated than older [cohort](## "gruppo, generazione")s.

## PERSONALITY PROFILE
Living with multi-sensory, multimedia content is reducing their [attention spans](## "capacità di attenzione"). On the other hand, they are [shaping up](## "svilupparsi, prendere forma") to be quick thinkers, capable of adapting to change. They are more aware of global issues than any previous generation. A strong sense of social justice and climate change is likely to push Gen Alpha [towards](## "verso") activism.

A sense of community is [coupled](## "combinare") with a need for self-expression. They are not just consumers of content; they are creators themselves — and not only for fun. In today’s world, being an influencer is a genuine career choice. Kids [raised](## "crescere") with iPhones and ChatGPT are already making money by gaming, [stream](## "flusso")ing and posting. They are spending it, too. Despite their young age, this [cohort](## "gruppo, generazione") has considerable influence on their families’ [purchasing](## "d’acquisto") habits.

## GENERATIONSHIPS
For Millennial parents, it is a constant struggle to manage screen time. ‘[Brain rot](## "marciume cerebrale")’ was added to the Oxford English Dictionary in 2024, defined as the “perceived loss of intelligence or critical thinking skills” due to the “overconsumption of unchallenging or inane content or material.” In other words, the [dumbing effect](## "effetto di impoverimento mentale") of endlessly watching social media videos. Ironically, the term was popularised on TikTok (although young users prefer YouTube shorts or gaming platforms like Roblox.)
In general, however, Gen Alphas get on well with their parents. They share their values and concerns about sustainability, health, technology, aesthetics, and quality, and they also tend to have good relationships with their older Gen Z [siblings](## "fratelli e sorelle").

## SIX-SEVEN!
Nevertheless, like every generation before them, they need to distinguish themselves [fr](## "davvero ('for real')")om other [cohort](## "gruppo, generazione")s, through their own, unique rituals and language. A host of new or [repurposed](## "riconvertire") expressions has entered the Gen Alpha lexicon.
In terms of rituals, the 6-7 phenomenon in particular has [flummoxed](## "confondere") their parents (and everyone else.) Thought to originate [fr](## "davvero ('for real')")om a song by the rapper Skrilla, it has become a viral meme. This [nonsense](## "assurda") exclamation is said with a special hand gesture and has kids [howling with laughter](## "ridere a crepapelle"). What does it mean? Absolutely nothing. It is like a secret [handshake](## "stretta di mano") that connects them and sets them apart, as they [claim](## "affermare") their place in the world. Another typical example is the Chicken Banana dance. Look it up online. It’s fire!
