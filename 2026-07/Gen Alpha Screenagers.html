NEW KIDS ON THE BLOCK

Generation Alpha was born between 2010 and 2025. The oldest are just finishing secondary education, while the youngest are still at pre-school age. They lived through the Covid pandemic and many of them experienced social distancing and remote schooling. Growing up with technology has given these ‘screenagers’ a high digital literacy, as well as a need for fast-paced, visual learning environments. They are exposed to a relentless stream of information, delivered via the internet to their devices, and this is shaping their perspective on life. It is no surprise that Gen Alpha whole-heartedly embraces artificial intelligence. Fortunately, they are better at detecting what is real and what is AI-generated than older cohorts.

GENERATIONSHIPS

For Millennial parents, it is a constant struggle to manage screen time. ‘Brain rot’ was added to the Oxford English Dictionary in 2024, defined as the “perceived loss of intelligence or critical thinking skills” due to the “overconsumption of unchallenging or inane content or material.” In other words, the dumbing effect of endlessly watching social media videos. Ironically, the term was popularised on TikTok (although young users prefer YouTube shorts or gaming platforms like Roblox.) In general, however, Gen Alphas get on well with their parents. They share their values and concerns about sustainability, health, technology, aesthetics, and quality, and they also tend to have good relationships with their older Gen Z siblings.

SIX-SEVEN!

Nevertheless, like every generation before them, they need to distinguish themselves from other cohorts, through their own, unique rituals and language. A host of new or repurposed expressions has entered the Gen Alpha lexicon. In terms of rituals, the 6-7 phenomenon in particular has flummoxed their parents (and everyone else.) Thought to originate from a song by the rapper Skrilla, it has become a viral meme. This nonsense exclamation is said with a special hand gesture and has kids howling with laughter. What does it mean? Absolutely nothing. It is like a secret handshake that connects them and sets them apart, as they claim their place in the world. Another typical example is the Chicken Banana dance. Look it up online. It’s fire!
