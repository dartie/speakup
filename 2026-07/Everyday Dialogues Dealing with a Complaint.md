James: Excuse me, I’m not happy with this product. It stopped working after a couple of days.
Laura: I’m really sorry to hear that. Let me see what I can do.
James: I paid [quite](## "abbastanza") a lot for it, so this is pretty frustrating.
Laura: I completely understand where you’re coming from. Do you happen to have the receipt with you?
James: Yes, I do. Here it is.
Laura: Alright, no problem at all. We can sort this out for you. We can offer you a [replacement](## "sostituzione") or give you a [full refund](## "rimborso completo").
James: To be honest, I’d [rather](## "piuttosto") get a refund.
Laura: That’s absolutely fine. I’ll take care of it right away. Could you hand over your credit card? I’ll have the full [amount](## "somma") charged back to your account.
James: Alright. I appreciate it.
Laura: There. All done.
James: Great! Thanks for your help.
Laura: No problem at all. Sorry for the inconvenience.

## NOW LET’S REVIEW THE VOCABULARY!
'Pretty' is the same as [quite](## "abbastanza") or [rather](## "piuttosto").
"I understand where you’re coming from" means that you recognise and accept someone’s point of view.
"Do you happen to…?" is a [polite](## "gentile") and indirect way to start a question.
'To sort out' means to resolve a problem.
"To be honest" (or TBH in texts) is a common phrase used to introduce a sincere but potentially [unwelcome](## "indesiderata") opinion.
"I’d [rather](## "piuttosto")…" is used to express a preference in a [polite](## "gentile") way.
When you 'fix a problem' or 'deal with a situation', you 'take care of it'.
'Right away' means immediately, without [delay](## "ritardo").
'To hand something over' means to give an object to another person.
'To charge back' refers to the reversal of a credit card transaction, to return money to the [card holder](## "titolare della carta")’s bank account.
"I appreciate it" is a [polite](## "gentile") expression of gratitude.
When you say, "All done", it means the issue has been completely resolved.
"Sorry for the inconvenience" is a [polite](## "gentile") way of [apologising](## "scusarsi") for any trouble that has been caused.

# Glossary

* **full refund** = rimborso completo
* **amount** = somma
* **unwelcome** = indesiderata
* **quite** = abbastanza
* **rather** = piuttosto
* **polite** = gentile
* **delay** = ritardo
* **card holder** = titolare della carta
* **apologising** = scusarsi
